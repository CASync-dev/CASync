CASync is the all in one timetable platform, where you can quickly sync your calendar via iCal and share with your friends, as well as add events to your already busy schedule.

Packaged with a quick overview to see your next classes and when your friends would be free, CASync is built for students to be organised and ready for their next event.

Some of our cool features...

Friends

Add your friends and easily view their timetables! No more asking for screenshots of timetables from your friends!

Groups

Create groups based on different projects or just create a group for your friends, and view a collective group timetable.

Schedule Customisation

Create your schedule via your iCal Link, or manually add some custom events of your own to make your schedule yours.
